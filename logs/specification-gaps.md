# MicroSim Specification Gaps

**Run:** MicroSims 2.0 batch generation, 2026-09-30 — 112 MicroSims built from the `#### Diagram:` specifications in chapters 1–26

**Source:** the `[spec-gap]` bullets in `logs/microsim-generation-2026-09-30/batch-01.md` … `batch-14.md`, written by the 14 agents that built the sims. Theme counts come from the hand-coded synthesis in `logs/microsim-generator-2026-09-30.md` §2.

A **specification gap** is a detail the chapter's specification left out, left ambiguous or got wrong, so the agent building the sim had to invent it, guess it or resolve a conflict. Each gap below records what was missing and, usually, what the agent chose.

## Summary

- **450 gaps in 112 of 112 sims.** Every sim had at least 2 and at most 7 (mean 4.0). No specification was complete.
- **Most gaps are missing content, not missing build details.** The largest theme is content the learner sees but the spec never supplied (145 bullets, 91 sims): card banks with 4 of 12 cards written, datasets with no values, "six objectives" with none given. Missing rules and thresholds (56 sims), interaction effects (53) and quiz mechanics (37) follow.
- **Many specs contradicted themselves or their chapter.** 34 sims had internal contradictions, mostly between layout paragraphs, and 29 disagreed with their own chapter, a cited source file or what the library can do.
- **The gaps changed the sims.** 55 of 112 sims (49%) departed from their specification as written, most often to make a narrow layout fit, to add the assessment the objective needed, or for accessibility.
- **Fix:** chapter-content-generator v1.11 adds objective-first specification rules (`references/microsim-specification-rules.md`, rules R1–R14) that target each theme below. The "Prevented by" column names the rule.

## Themes

### Content and data values not supplied

Specs named the kind of content but not the content: card banks, datasets, definitions, sample statements, places, presets. At least 22 sims asked for a bank of N items but supplied 0–4 of them.

- which-kind-of-object-classifier: only four of the twelve cards were given; the agent wrote the other eight
- type-routing-decision-tree: "six described objectives", none given
- chart-type-chooser: no dataset values, labels, units or questions; three datasets invented
- marker-popup-tile-map: places left to the author; coordinates entered from memory

### Rules, thresholds, models and ranges

A behavior was named without its rule: stop thresholds and whether they are inclusive, scoring formulas, the model behind a slider, slider min/max/step, and degenerate cases.

- evidence-threshold-lab: whether a threshold is inclusive
- fidelity-accuracy-base-rate-lab: a "signal" slider with no model behind it
- bkt-parameter-lab: P(S) = 0 divides by zero; which estimate is compared with the threshold

### Interaction semantics

An interaction was named without its effect or the state that follows: what a toggle toggles between, what one Step advances, what happens to a revealed answer when an input changes.

- p5-sketch-lifecycle-explorer: Step could mean one statement or one frame
- prerequisite-propagation-explorer: "Toggle threshold" with no values
- tenant-pseudonym-explorer: what changing the salt does after a derivation

### Assessment mechanics and feedback

A quiz, check or score without an item count, attempt policy, scoring rule, feedback text or reveal rule.

- microsim-family-tree: quiz size, example pool and wrong-answer feedback unspecified
- choose-the-type-challenge: scoring rule unspecified
- generation-failure-mode-triage: retry policy unspecified; feedback needed for up to 40 case/answer pairs

### Contradictions inside one spec

Layout, controls, interactions and responsive paragraphs disagreed, or numbers were inconsistent.

- class-mastery-heatmap-reader: light = low mastery in one place, dark = struggling in the objective
- canvas-height-calculator: a 200–900 slider with step 2 cannot show totals up to 1102
- tenant-pseudonym-explorer: drop-down in Layout, radio buttons in Controls

### Spec vs chapter, source files or library

The spec disagreed with its chapter's prose, with a real file it described, or with what the chosen library can do.

- choose-the-type-challenge: 12 type buttons, but the chapter discusses 13 types
- bouncing-ball-gravity-lab: 450 px in the Chapter 12 example, 500 px from its Chapter 1 spec
- quality-chain-workflow, ai-generation-pipeline: clickable Mermaid edges, which Mermaid cannot do

### Build details (canvas height, narrow widths, layout, control placement)

Specs either omitted these or prescribed layouts that could not fit. These are generator decisions; v1.11 removes them from specifications instead of adding fields for them.

- star-rating-comparison-table: a height formula that ignored two control rows
- microsim-family-tree: four boxes left to right gives about 9 px text at 400 px
- nine specs put controls inside the drawing region, which the p5 guide forbids

### Color, labels and defaults

Color with no stated meaning, missing units and quadrant names, and no default or reset state.

- prompt-text-rule-checker: green/red meant correct/incorrect or safe/risky?
- loop-polarity-tracer: the name "Loop B" gave away the balancing loop
- bkt-parameter-lab: what Reset restores

### Sources, objective fit and routing

Pointers to a source without a path, activities that could not produce the objective's evidence, and free-text library values the tools did not recognize.

- gateway-request-simulator: "mirror validation.py" with no path
- policy-precedence-resolver: five configuration layers but three dropdowns
- five specs gave library values such as "HTML and CSS" that fell back to p5.js
