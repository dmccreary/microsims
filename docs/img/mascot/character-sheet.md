# Character Sheet: Bounce the Ball

The canonical identity document for Bounce, the pedagogical mascot for the
**MicroSims 2.0: Generating, Instrumenting and Evaluating Interactive Learning Objects with AI**
textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated content involving
this character must re-anchor to the description below. It is the source of truth
for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Bounce
- **Species:** Ball
- **Subject:** MicroSims: AI-generated interactive simulations that measure learning
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's bounce it around!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Bright orange-red rubber ball with a small white highlight on the upper left — hex `#E8452C`
- **Accent color:** Bright blue (sneakers, dotted bounce arc, small headband) — hex `#1E88E5`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small blue sport headband, white sneakers with blue soles, and a faint dotted blue arc showing a bounce path. The ball has stubby cartoon arms and legs, which are needed for waving, pointing and thumbs-up gestures.
- **Expression:** Big friendly eyes, a wide open smile, and slightly raised eyebrows that show curiosity.
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, a round body about as wide as it is tall, suitable for icon-sized display at about 90 pixels.
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector cartoon, clean lines, and simple squash-and-stretch shapes.

## Personality

- Curious and energetic, always eager to try one more experiment
- Resilient: treats every mistake as a bounce that leads somewhere, and normalizes struggle
- Honest about evidence: says what the data show and does not overclaim
- Playful, with light physics jokes that never distract from the lesson

## Voice

- Short, upbeat sentences in plain language
- Physics and bounce puns used sparingly (rebound, momentum, bounce back), never more than one per admonition
- Addresses readers as "fellow builders" or simply "you"
- Signature phrases: "Let's bounce it around!", "Every bounce leaves evidence.", "Bounce back and try again!"

## Why This Mascot

The bouncing ball is the classic first MicroSim, and the opening example of the
original course, so Bounce carries the book's origin story. A ball is also a
natural metaphor for resilience (a mistake is just a bounce), and its bounce
path echoes the trail of events that a MicroSim leaves behind for learners. As
a simple geometric character it is easy to keep consistent across poses and
across AI image generators, and it is visually distinct from the robots, otter,
red panda, cat and turtle that are the mascots of the sibling books.
