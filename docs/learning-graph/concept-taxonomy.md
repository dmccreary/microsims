# Concept Taxonomy

The 462 concepts of *MicroSims 2.0* fall into 15 categories. The TaxonomyID is used in `learning-graph.csv`.

## Foundations and Anatomy

**TaxonomyID:** `FOUND`  
**Concepts:** 36

What a MicroSim is, the web technologies it rests on, and the files and regions that make up one.

## Learning Objectives and Bloom

**TaxonomyID:** `OBJ`  
**Concepts:** 26

Learning objectives, Bloom's 2001 Taxonomy, cognitive load, and the instructional design checkpoint that precedes any code.

## MicroSim Types and Routing

**TaxonomyID:** `TYPE`  
**Concepts:** 34

The MicroSim type catalog, the routing rubric that maps an objective to a type, and specialized types such as labs and sorting quizzes.

## AI Generation and Skills

**TaxonomyID:** `GEN`  
**Concepts:** 24

Generating MicroSims with AI skills and agents: prompts, specifications, refinement, and the failure modes of generated code.

## p5.js MicroSims

**TaxonomyID:** `P5`  
**Concepts:** 22

Building animated and interactive MicroSims with p5.js, including physics, controls and showcase-quality animation.

## Charts, Plots and Tables

**TaxonomyID:** `CHRT`  
**Concepts:** 20

Charts, plots, tables and infographics built with Chart.js, Plotly and HTML.

## Diagrams, Networks, Timelines and Maps

**TaxonomyID:** `DIAG`  
**Concepts:** 30

Diagrams, networks, causal-loop diagrams, timelines and maps built with Mermaid, vis-network, vis-timeline and Leaflet.

## Image Overlays and Posters

**TaxonomyID:** `OVL`  
**Concepts:** 28

Image overlays, grid-overlay comparison posters, and fact-verified posters.

## Responsive Design and Quality Assurance

**TaxonomyID:** `QA`  
**Concepts:** 36

Width-responsive design, iframe height management, and automated quality checks: the quality score, Playwright tests and vision-based layout review.

## Batch Generation, Metadata and Reuse

**TaxonomyID:** `BATCH`  
**Concepts:** 30

Batch generation from specifications, status tracking, metadata, search and reuse.

## xAPI Instrumentation

**TaxonomyID:** `XAPI`  
**Concepts:** 40

Instrumenting MicroSims with xAPI: statements, verbs, evidence classes, the runtime, and concept mapping.

## The Full LRS

**TaxonomyID:** `LRS`  
**Concepts:** 34

The full Learning Record Store: architecture, multi-tenancy, ingestion, storage, dashboards, cost and compliance.

## LRS-Lite

**TaxonomyID:** `LITE`  
**Concepts:** 28

LRS-Lite, the serverless compact strategy: producer-side summaries, the browser database, sync, and choosing between Full and Lite.

## Mastery Prediction and Evaluation

**TaxonomyID:** `MAST`  
**Concepts:** 40

Predicting concept mastery from the xAPI stream: knowledge tracing, and how to measure predictive fidelity.

## Pedagogy, Ethics and the Future

**TaxonomyID:** `PED`  
**Concepts:** 34

Pedagogy, accessibility, privacy and ethics, the capstone, and the future of MicroSims.
